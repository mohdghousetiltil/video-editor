Single clip and the Director
  • Auto lets the Director pick the model, length, resolution, sound, and whether to draw a picture first or enhance after, for the best quality within your budget.
  • You always see its plan, its reasons and the exact price before anything is charged.
  • I'll choose lets you pick a model yourself. The cost shown is an estimate from the model's price per second.
  • Add background music under a finished clip mixes in copyright-free music made on your server. It dips under any voices.
Story mode
  • Paste a story and the AI plans scenes, keeps characters consistent, voices it and stitches one video.
  • Auto (Recommended): the Director picks a model for each scene: the best ones for faces and key moments, cheaper ones elsewhere, within your total budget.
  • Keep characters consistent draws one character sheet and starts each scene from a picture made from it (about $0.02 per scene).
  • Picture first draws every scene as a still (about $0.02 each) so you can swap any that look off before paying for video.
  • Connect scenes ends each clip on the next scene's picture (on models with end-frame support: Veo, Seedance, Wan, Gemini), so cuts flow smoothly.
  • Budget mode uses the cheapest model (Grok Imagine 1.5 Lite, 480p, $0.02/s) and trims scenes to fit; the narration carries the story.
  • Chain scenes starts flagged scenes from the previous scene's last frame: more consistent, but those scenes run one after another.
  • Clips are made silent so the sound matches across scenes; narration and music are added when stitching.
  • Clips from different models can look slightly different.
Looks and character
  • Swipe the Look cards (Real, Disney, Pixar, Anime, Brain rot, None). The one in the middle is used.
  • The look and your character description are added word for word to every clip on Single clip and Story, so a series stays consistent.
  • Enhance character turns a rough idea into one exact description.
Shorts maker
  • The AI watches the whole video (long ones in 10-minute parts, then compares the best candidates) and cuts the strongest moment tight.
  • Story voiceover: a 1–2 s flash of the best moment with a hook line ("Wait till you see what happens"), then the story from the start. The video's own sound fades down whenever the narrator speaks.
  • Music: copyright-free tracks made on your server (Cinematic, Upbeat, Suspense, Chill, Epic, Funny). Auto picks the mood. It dips under speech.
  • Jump cuts only happen when it picks more than one piece; a short single-shot video gets zooms only. Zooms are skipped with "Clean" editing.
  • Direct links (.mp4, .mov, Dropbox, Google Drive) are fetched straight away. Page links (YouTube, X…) go through a downloader, which YouTube sometimes blocks; upload the file instead.
  • Pictures turns up to 10 pictures into a narrated explainer. Optionally pick an influencer cameo: they are drawn presenting up to 3 of the pictures (a picture model call each, shown in the cost), and your original follows each one.
  • Only use footage you have the rights to. Reposting other people's videos usually gets a Content ID claim or strike.
Influencers
  • Pictures first, video second: a picture costs 1–6 cents, so you lock the look cheaply before paying for video.
  • Video models block photos of real people. Make influencer from my photo creates a new AI character with your hair, skin tone and vibe: Realistic (Grok, Veo and most models) or 3D, Disney-like, Ghibli-like, Anime (every model, Seedance too).
  • Seedance also blocks realistic AI faces: pick a stylized look and press Test with Seedance on the card.
  • The description, seed and picture are saved exactly and reused in every video, so the character stays the same.
UGC review
  • Start frame combines the influencer and your product photo into one picture of them holding your exact product. The video starts from it, so the product matches.
  • Tips: a clear, front-facing influencer in good light, and a sharp product photo on a plain background. Generated text on packaging can warp, so check before using in ads.
  • Without the start frame, the influencer's saved picture is the first frame; the product photo can't be sent then, so describe the product.
Poses and copy motion
  • Each pose becomes its own short vertical clip you can drop into TikTok or CapCut.
  • Clips start from the influencer's saved picture, so face, outfit and room stay the same. Stay on 480p while testing.
  • Reference video (Wan 3.0 / Seedance) makes a new video following your clip's motion; Wan accepts realistic faces, Seedance only stylized ones. FLUX Video Edit swaps the person in your video: cheapest, but the face is only similar and it needs a description.
  • Only direct MP4 links work; YouTube, TikTok and Instagram page links don't, so save the clip and upload it. Motion copying is never frame-perfect.
  • Copy motion needs the video model to read your clip from a public address. Sharing starts a temporary tunnel that exposes only the clips you upload there, each behind a long random address. Pasting a public link doesn't need it.
Scripts
  • A chat with a suspense writer for original stories (not reaction content): hook, timed beats with what we see, twist, title ideas.
  • Use in Story mode sends just the spoken lines to Story. About 1–2 cents a reply.
Costs
  • Everything is charged to your own OpenRouter (or fal) credit; the app never charges you itself.
  • Cost shown on each page is an estimate. Paid steps ask you to confirm first, and History shows what each video actually cost.
  • When the monthly cap is reached, paid steps are refused until next month or until you raise it. For a hard limit on OpenRouter too, set a monthly limit on your key at openrouter.ai/settings/keys.
  • Cached clips, stitch folders and frames are cleared on a schedule (Settings). Download anything you want to keep.
  • Music, sound effects and Enhance run on your server and cost nothing.
